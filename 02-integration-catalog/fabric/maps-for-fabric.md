## In one sentence

ArcGIS Maps for Microsoft Fabric lets people create maps from Fabric data and
explore it with geographic context.

## Why a customer cares

* Reveal geographic patterns behind enterprise data
* Explore relationships that tables alone may obscure
* Connect the map conversation to an existing Fabric workflow

## Listen for this

> "We are moving enterprise analytics to Fabric."

> "We want to see our Fabric data on a map."

## Questions to ask

* Which Fabric data has a location component?
* Who needs to view maps, and who needs to create them?
* What question should geographic context help answer?
