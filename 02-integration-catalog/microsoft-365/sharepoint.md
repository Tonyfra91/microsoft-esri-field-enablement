## In one sentence

ArcGIS for SharePoint adds interactive maps and location context to SharePoint
lists and documents.

## Why a customer cares

* View list records in their geographic setting
* Find documents associated with a place
* Bring geographic information into an existing team site

## Listen for this

> "Our site information is stored in SharePoint lists."

> "People need to find documents by location."

## Questions to ask

* Which lists or documents relate to sites, assets, or service areas?
* What location information is present, and who maintains it?
* Who owns SharePoint permissions, installation, and ArcGIS access?
