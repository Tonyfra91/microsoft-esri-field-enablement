## In one sentence

ArcGIS for Teams brings interactive ArcGIS maps and content into Microsoft Teams
channels and meetings.

## Why a customer cares

* Give a team geographic context during a discussion
* Explore a shared map rather than rely only on a static picture
* Help colleagues discover relevant ArcGIS content where they collaborate

## Listen for this

> "Our users already live in Teams."

> "People need to discuss the same places together."

## Questions to ask

* Which team decision would benefit from a shared map?
* Is the relevant content public or restricted to your ArcGIS organization?
* Who can approve the app and validate access for meeting participants?

## See it

Use the official [present and share walkthrough](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/teams/latest/workflows/present-and-share-in-arcgis-for-teams.htm).
Confirm the participants' access before preparing a demonstration.
