## In one sentence

This conversation assesses whether an Azure hosting approach can meet the
customer's ArcGIS Enterprise requirements.

## Conversation card

**30-second explanation:** Begin with what the GIS platform must deliver for
the business. Bring Azure and GIS specialists together to assess whether a
supported hosting design can meet those requirements.

**Do not promise:** A validated Azure topology, government-cloud support,
resilience, performance, or compliance from this discovery page.

**Agree on a next step:** Ask the GIS service owner, Azure architect, and Esri
specialist to record versions, recovery objectives, constraints, and the public
support evidence still needed. Use the
[technical checklist](../../05-architecture/technical-qualification.md).

## Why a customer cares

* Align GIS infrastructure with cloud strategy
* Clarify availability and recovery requirements
* Establish operational ownership before an investment

These are assessment goals, not verified deployment capabilities.

## Listen for this

> "Our GIS needs to run in Azure."

> "We need to define recovery requirements for the GIS platform."

> "Cloud and GIS teams need to agree on operational responsibility."

## Questions to ask

* Which ArcGIS Enterprise version and workloads are involved?
* What availability, recovery, residency, and network requirements apply?
* Who will operate, secure, license, and support the environment?
