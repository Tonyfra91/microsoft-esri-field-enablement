## Start with a repeated task

Ask where staff repeatedly look up locations, enrich records, or move information
between a business process and GIS. The first-pass focus is
[ArcGIS Connectors for Power Automate](power-automate.md).

The customer value to evaluate is less manual handling of location-related
steps, not a guaranteed productivity saving.

## Keep the scope clear

This folder does not describe a native "ArcGIS for Power Apps" product.
A connector listing across Microsoft services does not by itself establish
such a product. AI, agents, and Copilot extensibility are outside this first pass.

## Learn more

* [Esri: ArcGIS Connectors for Power Automate](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/power-automate/latest/get-started/get-started-with-arcgis-connectors-for-power-automate.htm)
* [Microsoft: ArcGIS connector reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/connectors/arcgis/)

Sources reviewed: 2026-10-27. Confirm the exact connector, supported environment,
authentication, and licensing before proposing a flow.
