## Customer conversation flow

Curated field guidance, not an official product positioning statement.

Five minutes before a meeting? Use the
[Seller Quick Start](../01-start-here/seller-quick-start.md) and
[integration navigator](../README.md#explore-the-integrations).

1. Ask what decision the customer needs to improve and why location matters.
2. Understand the current workflow with the
   [discovery questions](../01-start-here/customer-discovery-questions.md).
3. Introduce a [Microsoft and Esri integration](../02-integration-catalog/README.md)
   only when it matches the need.
4. Agree on a [demonstration](../04-demos/README.md) and
   [architecture review](../05-architecture/README.md) with clear evaluation criteria.
5. Close with an owner, next action, and authoritative documentation to review.

Start with the [Government scenario](../03-scenarios/government.md) when preparing
an Esri subsidiary or distributor conversation about government customers.

## Prepare, demonstrate, and follow up

| Need | Use |
|------|-----|
| A short explanation, customer signals, questions, and what not to promise | The conversation card on each [integration page](../02-integration-catalog/README.md) |
| Help choosing among similar integrations | [Workflow comparisons](../02-integration-catalog/compare-integrations.md) |
| A prepared meeting example | [Excel and Power BI runbooks](../04-demos/README.md), currently drafted and not product-tested |
| Technical preparation and role-based handoff | [Qualification checklist](../05-architecture/technical-qualification.md) |
| An end-to-end customer discussion | [Government, asset operations, and site planning scenarios](../03-scenarios/README.md) |
| A clear next action | [Follow-up email, technical handoff, and evaluation plan](meeting-templates.md) |

The business owner defines the decision. The Microsoft workload owner and GIS
owner check the proposed workflow with the appropriate solution engineer.
Add security, licensing, data, cloud, or development specialists as required.
Use roles rather than named personal contacts.

## When an answer is not established

Use: "We need to validate that requirement against current Microsoft and Esri
documentation before making a recommendation."

Do not promise licensing entitlements, deployment availability, compliance,
roadmap delivery, support arrangements, or commercial outcomes without evidence.
Do not publish private opportunity details or meeting notes here.

Find public learning links and sharing rules in
[partner enablement](../07-resources/partner-enablement.md).
Approved public pitch decks and customer references: **Needs current-source validation**.
