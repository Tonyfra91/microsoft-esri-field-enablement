## Start with the customer outcome

For quick meeting preparation, use the [Seller Quick Start](seller-quick-start.md)
or the [integration navigator](../README.md#explore-the-integrations).

Curated field guidance: begin with the decision a customer needs to make,
the people involved, and the role of location in that decision. Then explore
whether a documented Microsoft + Esri integration fits.

A suggested opening:

> Which decisions would benefit from seeing your business information in its
> geographic context, and how do your teams make those decisions today?

## Ground the conversation in an example

ArcGIS for Power BI adds mapping capabilities to Power BI reports and dashboards.
This statement is supported by
[Microsoft documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/visuals/power-bi-visualizations-arcgis)
and [Esri documentation](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/power-bi/latest/get-started/introduction-to-arcgis-for-power-bi.htm).
Sources reviewed: 2026-10-27.

Whether it fits a specific customer depends on their requirements and the
documented product constraints. Do not infer that every ArcGIS capability is
available through this integration.
