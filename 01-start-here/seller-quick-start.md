## What is ArcGIS + Microsoft

Bring location intelligence into the tools employees already use. Microsoft
supports everyday work and enterprise data; ArcGIS adds geographic context.
Together, the conversation is about business data, operational data, and location,
not only maps.

## Why should I care

Customers may know what happened without understanding where it happened or why
place matters. Ask whether geographic context could improve a decision about
service coverage, assets, demand, or operations. Treat the improvement as a
hypothesis to evaluate, not a promised result.

## What should I ask my customer

* Which decision or process needs to improve?
* Where do people already work, and where does the location data live?
* Who needs the result beyond the GIS team?
* What safe dataset and measurable goal could we use for an evaluation?
* Who must validate access, licensing, privacy, and environment requirements?

Use the [discovery questions](customer-discovery-questions.md) for a fuller conversation.

## What do I show them

For everyday work, open the
[Microsoft 365 learning path](https://learn.arcgis.com/en/paths/try-arcgis-for-microsoft/)
and choose the relevant exercise. For reporting, use Microsoft's
[ArcGIS map in Power BI walkthrough](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/visuals/power-bi-visualizations-arcgis).
These are official learning resources, not repository-owned or customer-validated demos.
Sources reviewed: 2026-10-27.

Check prerequisites first. Show one question with public or synthetic data;
do not use customer information in this public repository.
More options: [demo guidance](../04-demos/README.md).

## Who do I involve next

Bring the customer's business owner together with their GIS owner and an Esri
solution engineer. Involve the Microsoft account or partner team and the owner
of the relevant Microsoft workload. Add identity, security, cloud, or data
specialists when the requirements call for them.

Close with one evaluation question, an owner, and a next meeting.
Use the [architecture questions](../05-architecture/README.md) for that handoff,
not as the opening pitch.
