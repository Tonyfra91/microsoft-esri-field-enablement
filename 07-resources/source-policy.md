## Prefer authoritative current sources

Use this order when selecting evidence:

1. Current Esri product documentation
2. Current Esri product pages
3. Microsoft Learn
4. Microsoft Marketplace
5. Current Esri blogs
6. Current official Microsoft blogs
7. Current official Esri videos and demos
8. Approved publicly shareable partner materials

Use product documentation to establish product behavior. A blog, video, or
partner deck can illustrate a conversation but cannot override current support
requirements. Employee commentary is not authoritative product documentation.

## Keep information fresh

Prefer 2026 sources where available. Use 2025 sources when they remain current.
A maintained, version-appropriate documentation page may still be the authority
even when its publication date is older. Avoid older enablement content when
newer product information exists.

Never reuse an old screenshot, product name, architecture, roadmap statement,
licensing statement, UI image, or demo merely because it is available.
If information is stale, conflicting, inaccessible, or cannot be verified, mark it:

**Needs current-source validation**

Do not fabricate an update or turn missing evidence into a capability claim.

## Record what was actually checked

For each technical claim or learning resource:

* Link the original public source beside the claim or in the page's source list
* Record the source review date and any relevant version or environment scope
* Record the source's publication or update date when available
* Distinguish the review date from the publication date and deployment test date
* Check the destination after redirects, not only whether a URL responds
* Flag preview, retirement, availability, licensing, or support boundaries

An undated product page can be cited with a review date, but do not label it
"published in 2026." A successful page fetch is not proof of product suitability.
No shared maturity or general-availability status is implied by catalog placement.

## Separate evidence from seller guidance

Product descriptions must be source-backed. Customer signals, suggested
questions, and business-value statements are curated guidance and hypotheses
to test. They are not customer references or guarantees of cost savings,
compliance, performance, or production readiness.

Confirm accounts, prerequisites, availability, licensing, sharing, and support
for the customer's actual environment before making a recommendation.
Government-cloud support requires explicit current evidence.

## Keep public content public-safe

Do not publish confidential Microsoft or Esri material, internal roadmaps,
private meeting notes, private pricing, customer-sensitive data, internal
SharePoint URLs, internal screenshots, or unpublished claims.

Treat an internal lead only as a reason to search for authoritative public
evidence. If none exists, omit the claim. Do not copy internal wording into a
public placeholder.

Public accessibility is not permission to redistribute an image or deck.
Follow the [visual asset rules](visual-assets.md) and
[partner sharing rules](partner-enablement.md).

## Before contributing

Check source currency, local navigation, public sharing status, and asset rights.
Follow [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Return to the
[catalog](../02-integration-catalog/README.md) or [seller front door](../README.md).
