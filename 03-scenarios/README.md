## Choose a familiar question

These are hypothetical examples, not customer references or validated solutions.
Success criteria are proposed evaluation tests, not achieved business outcomes.

| Conversation | Business question | Starting path |
|--------------|-------------------|---------------|
| [Government services](government.md) | What information is missing when we compare service demand and locations? | Power BI report, then a reviewed evaluation |
| [Asset operations](asset-operations.md) | Can staff connect an asset location to the right supporting information? | Excel demonstration, then the appropriate content workflow |
| [Site planning](site-planning.md) | What information do we need before comparing possible sites? | Excel exploration, then qualified analysis |

Each example identifies data, people, a demonstration, technical checks, and
criteria for proceeding or pausing. Keep customer-specific answers in an
approved private system. Use the
[meeting templates](../06-sales-enablement/meeting-templates.md) to agree on the next step.
